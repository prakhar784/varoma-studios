07 / FAQ

Questions,
answered.

How long does a business website take?

Timeline depends on pages, content, revisions, integrations and functionality. A focused brochure-style project can be planned in a few weeks.

Can the website include lead forms?

Yes. Forms can later connect to email, CRM, database, WhatsApp or another workflow.

Can you build a web application?

Yes. A website can be extended with authentication, dashboards, APIs, databases and custom business logic.

Will it work on mobile?

Yes. This demo uses responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile.

How is the final price decided?

Final pricing depends on confirmed scope, design complexity, functionality, integrations, content and support.
